## Style
- plural_categories: one, few, many, other
- plural_note: one: 1, 21, 31 and so on (not 11); few: 2-4, 22-24 and so on (not 12-14); many: 0, 5-20 and the rest; other: fractions. Every counted noun needs all four.
- cases: 6 cases. Numerals govern case (5 приёмников, 2 приёмника, 1 приёмник). Use Fluent terms with case attributes. Abbreviations and kept-English words do not decline: use a carrier noun (сигнал ГНСС, режим holdover). Use 'ё' consistently (приёмник, лётной).
- register: Formal-neutral technical: 'вы' in lower case when address is unavoidable, polite plural imperative (Запустите); impersonal constructions in prose. Standard technical terminology only; no political or topical content.
- separators: Decimal comma; thousands separator a non-breaking space; Intl formats these. Quotation marks « » (nested „ “). Em dash with spaces as in the source.
