## The B4 miss

The kshana−Orekit difference is the same in each noiseless scenario and its noisy twin (e.g.
rr_leo 1.464e-2 m in both), and it is Orekit's estimate, not kshana's, that is away from the truth:
kshana recovers the simulated truth to ≤ 1.1e-8 m (B5) and its noiseless post-fit residuals are at
rounding level, while Orekit's noiseless post-fit residual RMS is 1.4 cm (range), 7–9e-5 m/s
(range-rate) and 4e-10 to 2.6e-8 rad (angles) on data its own propagator simulated. A post-hoc diagnostic
(driver run with `-Dod.threshold=1e-14` instead of the committed `1e-9`; fixture not changed)
gives the identical Orekit estimates (same Δ to 4 digits, same iteration counts), so it is not the
stopping threshold. The cause on the Orekit side was not isolated; a plausible candidate is how
`BatchLSEstimator` evaluates the fixed-step RK4 trajectory at the (gapped) measurement dates
versus the simulator's direct `propagate(t_k)`. The existing range-only comparison, with a
measurement at every step, agreed to 1e-3 m.

Recorded as a miss. The test pins it: exactly `rr_leo` and `mixed_leo` miss B4, by at most
2e-2 m / 1.2e-5 m/s (a regression bound on the recorded miss, not a pass bar); if either starts
passing, the test fails until this file is updated.

## Claim

VALIDATED against Orekit 12.2 for this teaching model (two-body + J2, fixed inertial stations,
instantaneous geometric measurements): the elevation-mask visibility, the range/range-rate/az-el
measurement values, the variational STM (to 1.2e-14), and the noisy-data batch estimate and
per-type post-fit RMS. The noiseless kshana–Orekit state agreement is 1.6 mm to 1.7 cm, outside the
pre-registered 1 cm on two scenarios, for the reason above.
