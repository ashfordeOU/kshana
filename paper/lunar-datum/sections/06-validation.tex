\section{Validation and the structure/magnitude firewall}
\label{sec:validation}

A paper that reports lunar decimetres must be scrupulous about which of its numbers
are earned and which are illustrative. We enforce this with an explicit firewall,
implemented as a rule in the engine's verification suite and stated plainly for the
reader: a quantity is \Validated{} only when it is checked against an
\emph{independent} oracle---a separate dataset, library, or published vector---to a
stated tolerance; everything else is \Modelled{}. This section states what falls on
each side and gives the external check that anchors the \Validated{} side.

\subsection{An independent oracle for the decomposition}
\label{sec:scipy}

The load-bearing linear algebra of this paper is the decomposition of
Definition~\ref{def:metrics}: from a $7\times7$ Fisher matrix, form the
$\{0,3\}$ Schur complement \eqref{eq:schur} and extract $\mu$, $c_x$, and $\corr$.
We validate this computation against an independent SciPy/NumPy reference. A
generator script builds a deterministic set of $16$ synthetic $7\times7$
symmetric positive-definite matrices spanning well-conditioned to
$\{0,3\}$-near-degenerate regimes (including $\Fisher_{03}=\Fisher_{30}=\varrho$
for $\varrho\in\{0.90,0.97,0.99,0.995\}$, mirroring the closed-form stress case),
computes the Schur complement, its smallest eigenvalue, and its $2\times2$ inverse
using \code{numpy.linalg.inv} and \code{scipy.linalg.eigvalsh}, and emits the
expected $(\mu,c_x,|\corr|)$ to a vendored fixture (NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.18.0). The
engine's \code{decompose} reproduces every value to a maximum relative residual
below $10^{-9}$---double-precision agreement between two independent
implementations. This is the paper's one \Validated{} row: the decomposition
\emph{linear algebra} is externally confirmed, independent of any lunar modelling.

\subsection{What is Validated and what is Modelled}
\label{sec:matrix}

Table~\ref{tab:honesty} draws the line explicitly. The proven structure of
Section~\ref{sec:classification}, the improvement \emph{direction} of
Section~\ref{sec:radial}, and the greedy-vs-oracle \emph{agreement} of
Section~\ref{sec:greedy} are established by engine-internal, analytic, or
brute-force checks; they are honest but not independent, so they are recorded as
\Modelled{} with an internal-consistency oracle. Only the decomposition against
SciPy is \Validated{}. No lunar \emph{magnitude}---not the $0.9934$ correlation,
not the CRLB values, not the degeneracy-metric numbers, not the relative
costs---is \Validated{}, and none is claimed to reproduce the $-0.97$ or the
$15$~cm origin figure of \citet{sosnica2025ilrf}; those are the empirical anchor
whose \emph{structure} we explain, not a target we fit.

\subsection{Derivative and consistency checks}
\label{sec:derivchecks}

Every analytic sensitivity row \eqref{eq:sensrow} is cross-checked against a
finite-difference evaluation of its own measurement function. The LLR row
reproduces the independent four-parameter Phase-0 datum row exactly; the
two-station VLBI differential-delay row agrees with finite differences to a maximum
relative residual of $8.2\times10^{-8}$ (the residual is largest on the
weakest-signal rotation column, the fingerprint of floating-point cancellation in
the near-differenced delay, not of an analytic error); and the orbiter range row
agrees to below $10^{-8}$. The single-observation rank count, the defect lift under
real libration, and the monotonicity of $\mu$ in arc length are each asserted in
the continuous-integration suite. These checks confirm the derivatives and the
geometric structure are implemented correctly; they are internal, and we label them
so.

\subsection{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

Every quantitative result in this paper---the defect count, the LLR-only baseline,
the marginal gains and their $8.0\times$ ratio, the per-parameter CRLB fractions,
and the four Pareto-frontier points---is regenerated from the committed engine by a
single command (Section~\ref{sec:availability}). The three figures are produced by
a committed script from the same engine outputs. The DE440 orientation series, the
retroreflector coordinates, and the SciPy reference fixture are vendored with
provenance notices and SHA-256 checksums. There are no hand-entered results in the
tables: the manuscript numbers and the engine outputs are the same numbers.
