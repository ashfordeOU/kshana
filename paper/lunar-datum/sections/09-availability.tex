\label{sec:availability}
The datum-identifiability substrate is part of the open-source \code{kshana}
engine \citep{baweja2026kshana} (\url{https://github.com/ashfordeOU/kshana};
AGPL-3.0-only). The seven-parameter datum partials, the Schur decomposition, the
multi-technique combiner, and the OED optimizer are in modules
\code{lunar\_datum}, \code{lunar\_identifiability}, and \code{lunar\_oed}. Every
number in this paper is regenerated by
\begin{quote}
  \code{cargo run -{}-example p1\_datum\_identifiability}
\end{quote}
which prints the single-observation defect, the LLR-only baseline
$(\mu,\corr,c_x)$, the marginal gains and their ratio, the per-parameter CRLB
fractions, and the four Pareto-frontier points. The three figures are produced from
those outputs by \code{paper/lunar-datum/figures/gen\_figures.py}. The independent
SciPy/NumPy oracle is \code{scripts/gen\_datum\_identifiability\_ref.py}, its output
vendored with a provenance notice and SHA-256 at
\code{tests/fixtures/datum\_identifiability/}. The DE440 orientation series and the
retroreflector coordinates are vendored with notices at
\code{tests/fixtures/llr\_geometry/}, which also records the representative
(\Modelled) OED beacons, orbit, noise, and costs. All classification and
decomposition claims are asserted in the continuous-integration test suite.
