Open core

The whole validated engine and every surface, free with no feature gate. Everything on this site runs on it.

Why everyone needs it

Anyone who takes position or time from GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite Systems) is exposed: jamming, spoofing and signal loss reach vehicles, networks, grids and spacecraft alike. The open core shows how exposed, and says which figures are checked.

Who it is for
  • Engineers of vehicles, networks, grids and spacecraft that take position or time from GNSS
  • Researchers, students and reviewers who need figures they can reproduce and cite
  • Developers wiring the engine into code, a CI (continuous integration) suite or an AI (artificial intelligence) assistant
What is included
  • All 93 scenario kinds and 183 bundled scenarios: native, Python, JavaScript, in the browser and over MCP (Model Context Protocol)
  • The full verification matrix: 176 of 413 capabilities validated against independent oracles, the rest labelled Modelled
  • Every result stamped with scenario hash, seed and engine version, and written as JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), CSV (comma-separated values) tables and SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) charts
  • The free kshana --study suite runner
Terms

Free under the AGPL-3.0. The same core is also offered under a commercial licence, for closed integration into proprietary products (below).

Open Kshana runs one scenario and tells you what it gives. Kshana Pro answers the questions a programme asks next: which design to fly, how sure the answer is, and whether the mission meets its requirements in a form a review board can check.

Who it is for

What is included

  • Confidence and risk: confidence bands, the probability of meeting each limit and the inputs that matter most

Kshana Pro · the professional edition

From a simulation to a decision you can defend.

Kshana Pro is the professional edition of Kshana, for teams that have to choose a design, say how sure they are and hand the evidence to a review board. It runs on the same open engine and the same scenario files, adds the tools a programme needs on top, and comes with support and licensing under contract.

Who it is for

  • Mission and system architects who choose between designs and have to show why one was picked.
  • Programme and verification managers who report to a review, a customer or a procurement board.
  • Timing, navigation and hardware teams who need models of their own devices and systems.
  • Engineering organisations that keep their studies and their data on their own network.

What Kshana Pro does for you

Design exploration

Which design should we fly?

State the choices you have and the goals that matter. Kshana Pro explores the whole design space and shows the best trade-offs, so you can see what each extra satellite or plane buys. Every recommended design comes back as an ordinary scenario file that anyone can re-run with the free engine.

Confidence and risk

How sure are we, and what drives the result?

Say how uncertain your inputs are. Kshana Pro gives confidence bands, the probability of meeting each limit and the inputs that matter most, so a review sees a range instead of one optimistic number.

Mission dossier

Does the mission meet its requirements, and can we hand that over?

One command checks a programme's requirements against engine runs and produces a mission dossier: a verification matrix with its open items and a PDF (Portable Document Format) report in which every number traces back to a run.

Requirements and traceability

Which requirements does a change touch?

Bring requirements in from your systems-engineering tools and get a verification cross-reference matrix (VCRM), a Systems Modeling Language version 2 (SysML v2) model, a change-impact report and an offline evidence pack.

Regression watch

Did the last change make a figure we care about worse?

Name the figures that matter. Kshana Pro stops your pipeline when a change to a scenario, a model or the engine makes one of them worse, and keeps a tamper-evident history of every check.

Spectrum coexistence

Will our signal plan interfere with GNSS, or suffer from it?

Screen candidate signal plans against the open-service GNSS signals in both directions and get a verdict for each plan and each signal against the limit your study sets.

Trade studies

Which architecture is best on the measure we care about?

Rank architecture options on the figure of merit you choose, with every option reproducible from its own scenario.

Clock digital twins

How will our own clock hold time?

A digital twin of a clock that behaves like the device's published stability, checked before it is used. A twin of your own device is built with you, from your data, under contract and a non-disclosure agreement.

Technical notes

Can one run become a document we can share?

One scenario becomes a reproducible technical note in HTML (HyperText Markup Language) and JSON (JavaScript Object Notation): scope, method, results with an evidence label on every figure, limitations and the exact scenario run.

On-premises service

Can the team queue studies on its own network?

Run Kshana Pro as a service inside your network: the team queues studies, they run unattended, and you can show afterwards which result was delivered for which request.

Signed evidence

Can the receiver trust what we hand over?

Kshana Pro signs its evidence packs and dossiers. Whoever receives one can check that nothing changed since it was signed, and with which key, without a Pro licence.

Why teams choose Kshana Pro

  • Decisions, not single runs.The free engine answers one scenario at a time. Kshana Pro answers the questions a programme asks next.
  • Evidence a reviewer can check.Every figure keeps the label the open engine gave it (Validated, Modelled or Partner), deliverables are signed, and every result can be re-run with the free engine.
  • Support from the team that builds the engine.Onboarding, help with your scenarios and attention to the issues that block your programme, on the terms of your contract.
  • Licensed under contract.Kshana Pro comes with the warranty, indemnity and support terms agreed in your contract.
  • Runs where your data is.On an engineer's workstation or on your own network; nothing has to be uploaded.
  • The open engine stays free.Kshana Pro is built on the open core and never forks it, so nothing is taken out of the free engine.
  • A licence is required. Without one, Kshana Pro runs open scenarios only.
  • Modelled, and labelled so. Every Kshana Pro result is a modelled engineering result, not a certification, a qualification or an acceptance by any authority.
  • Your own devices, under contract. Models of your own hardware are built only with your data, under contract and a non-disclosure agreement.

02Custom study · how it runs

When you need an answer, not a tool.

A custom study uses the same engine you can run yourself, so every figure in it can be checked by anyone.

  1. Tell us the question. Mail contact@ashforde.org with the system and the decision it feeds. There is no form.
  2. We scope it. What will be modelled, with which scenarios, and what stays out of scope, under the appropriate clearance and NDA where the work needs it.
  3. We model it with the same engine. Every figure is labelled Validated or Modelled, exactly as on this site.
  4. You get a reproducible result. Reproducible from scenario, seed and engine version. A modelled study, not a certification.

Not the same as kshana --study: that free command runs a suite of scenarios you write into one stamped report (Developers). A custom study is paid work we do for you.
