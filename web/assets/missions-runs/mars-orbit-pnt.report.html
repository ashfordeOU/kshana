1. Executive summary

Positioning around any solar-system body: an orbiter or a surface lander around a central body chosen by name (Mars, the Moon, Europa, Ganymede, Titan and the rest of the solar-system catalogue), navigating with one-way pseudoranges from a small Walker navigation constellation around the body (two-body orbits with the body's own J2 secular drift, an unknown user clock) and optionally a clock-free deep-space two-way range from the Earth, whose direction and distance come from the analytic solar-system ephemeris at every epoch.
