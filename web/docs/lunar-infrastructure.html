Lunar orbit and surface as navigation and geodetic infrastructure

Scenario kind lunar-infrastructure, five studies selected by study. MODELLED: a restricted three-body model with stated simplifications and ILLUSTRATIVE inputs. The numerics are checked against independent numpy/scipy programs; nothing is compared with a measurement of a lunar quantity and nothing here is a statement about any mission or system. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any agency. Advisory software, not type-approved equipment. Kshana transmits nothing and drives no hardware.

The designs, inputs and bars were fixed in xval/lunar-infra/PREREGISTRATION.md (committed before any study was scored) and two disclosed amendments (PREREGISTRATION-amendment-1.md). The verdict of every bar is in xval/lunar-infra/RESULTS.md.

Study Scenario What it computes
od-consistency lunar-infrastructure-od-consistency.toml, ...-model-error.toml Onboard Earth-GNSS orbit determination in an NRHO (or family = "elfo") against independent ground range and range-rate OD from three DSN stations; each estimate's formal covariance; a seeded Monte Carlo of whether the two covariances explain their difference (q, chi-square with 6 degrees of freedom); an injected unmodelled acceleration
carrier-phase lunar-infrastructure-carrier-phase.toml (quick), xval/lunar-infra/scenarios/carrier-phase-scored.toml (scored, about eight minutes) Joint surface-station and orbiter double-differenced carrier phase; float ambiguity covariance; LAMBDA; bootstrapped and Monte Carlo success; float and fixed position sigma
frame-coestimation lunar-infrastructure-frame-coestimation.toml Fisher information of the 7-parameter lunar datum from Earth-station ranging, with and without orbiter-to-surface-station ranges; seeded random station sets
space-tie lunar-infrastructure-space-tie.toml GNSS, VLBI and laser ranging on one spacecraft: Cramer-Rao precision of the inter-technique offsets against the local-tie error
geodesy-sensitivity lunar-infrastructure-geodesy-sensitivity.toml Information on h2, k2, l2 and the physical libration from laser ranging, orbiter tracking, a gravimeter and a surface clock (constants shared with the lunar-time modules)

Stated simplifications#

Earth and Moon point masses on a circular orbit, no Sun, no lunar gravity harmonics; the Earth's rotation axis is the orbit normal; the inertial frame equals the rotating frame at t = 0; a spherical Moon; a synchronous lunar frame (no libration) except in geodesy-sensitivity; the orbit is held known in the lunar axes in frame-coestimation (its error is carried by the orbiter range sigma); the orbiter family of geodesy-sensitivity sees the line-of-sight integral of the k2 acceleration after a constant and a linear trend are removed, an upper-end figure.

Report shape#

Every report carries kind, study, label, scenario_hash, the study's fields and a units block (a unit and a provenance class for every numeric field). No per-time series are emitted. Field lists: the units block of each report, and docs/field-units-schema.json.

Sources#

k2 = 0.024116 +/- 0.000108 (Lemoine et al., Geophys. Res. Lett. 41(10), 3382-3389, 2014, doi 10.1002/2014GL060027), used as a nominal. h2 and l2 are linearisation points, not verified values. DSN station coordinates are the ITRF93 values in the NAIF earthstns_fx kernel comments; DSN accuracy bands were read from search summaries only. The space-tie concept follows the public GRASP and ESA Genesis descriptions. The full table, with how each was read, is in the pre-registration.

Results at a glance (see RESULTS.md)#

Not met, and pinned by tests that assert the miss: C3 (the injected-error metric does not fire at 3e-10 m/s2), the bootstrapped-rate half of Q3 at 1e-9, and the CRLB-trace half of G3. Integer ambiguity fixing is not achieved at the registered inputs. Nothing is VALIDATED.

Kshana · क्षण · the precise instant

Open-source PNT (positioning, navigation and timing) resilience simulator. It drives no radio hardware and transmits nothing: its signal-level output is baseband IQ sample files, and its software GNSS receiver is an analysis tool. Not a flight product.
