holdover-test (scenario kind)

Scenario kind of the engine v0.39.0, family Clocks, timing and holdover. Advisory: analysis output from a research and engineering tool, not type-approved navigation equipment and not a safety-of-life service. Every label here is the engine's or the verification matrix's; a kind is MODELLED unless the matrix says otherwise (how a label is earned: the README's Evidence section).

What it does#

MODELLED holdover test of a timing system, scored from a time-error record the OPERATOR supplies (a linuxptp ptp4l, phc2sys or ts2phc log, a chrony tracking or statistics log, a TICC, TimeLab or RINEX clock log, or time_s,time_error_ns pairs). The test has four steps and Kshana performs none of them: a locked BASELINE, GNSS REMOVED by the operator (the antenna cable or the simulator output; Kshana transmits and drives nothing) at a stated time, HOLDOVER on the system's own oscillator, and RECOVERY after the operator restores GNSS at a second stated time. The record is split at those two times. Baseline: time-error statistics and the ITU-T masks written for a locked clock. Holdover: statistics, the fractional frequency offset the system held (a least-squares line), the masks written for holdover or an autonomous clock, the ePRTC-A holdover envelope from the stated locked duration, and the time to exceed each time-error budget. Recovery: whether and when |TE| came inside a stated band and stayed there, the peak and the largest step. Every limit is transcribed from a public ITU-T table (the telecom-timing masks); the words are 'measured against the mask', and the two times are the operator's statement, not verified. A record too short for a mask is INCOMPLETE, never extrapolated. See docs/TIMING-HIL.md.

One run of scenarios/holdover-test-ptp4l.toml summarises as: holdover-test: holdover 1795 s, max|TE| 59.0 ns; prtc-a PASS, eprtc-a-holdover FAIL; recovered 0 s after GNSS returned (MODELLED, measured against the mask)

Limitations#

What the run's own report says is not modelled or not validated:

  • MODELLED — a holdover test scored from a time-error record the operator supplied. Every limit is transcribed from the named public ITU-T Recommendation and the result is measured against the mask; it says nothing about whether any clock or system meets a Recommendation, and nobody has rated or approved it. The times at which GNSS was removed and restored are the operator's statement and are not verified. Advisory sof (result label)
  • MODELLED holdover test of a timing system, scored from a time-error record the OPERATOR supplies (a linuxptp ptp4l, phc2sys or ts2phc log, a chrony tracking or statistics log, a TICC, TimeLab or RINEX clock log, or time_s,time_error_ns pairs). (kind catalogue (kshana kinds --json))
  • Reproducibility & software assurance is MODELLED, not validated: a sub-claim is externally checked, but the whole capability composes modelled pieces, so the capability stays Modelled. (verification matrix (docs/MODELLED-RATIONALE.md))
  • ITU-T telecom synchronisation masks with PASS/FAIL and margin is MODELLED, not validated: checked against its own closed-form / analytic identity — catches transcription and coefficient errors, but is not an external oracle. (verification matrix (docs/MODELLED-RATIONALE.md))
  • Holdover test scored from an operator-supplied time-error record is MODELLED, not validated: checked against its own closed-form / analytic identity — catches transcription and coefficient errors, but is not an external oracle. (verification matrix (docs/MODELLED-RATIONALE.md))
