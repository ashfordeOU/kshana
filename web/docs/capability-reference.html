Per satellite and per epoch it computes the off-boresight angle against the transmitter nadir, whether the Earth occults the straight path, the slant range and its free-space loss, the transmit gain at that angle, the received power and the carrier-to-noise density; it then aggregates only the links clearing a tracking threshold, and sweeps a full constellation revolution so the answer is a DISTRIBUTION rather than one snapshot.
