Scenario kinds, by family

Every scenario kind the engine v0.38.0 dispatches (87), filed under 12 families. Each family has a page with one entry per kind (the engine's description and fields, the example command, headline numbers from a run, the matrix rows that name it, and the chart the engine writes) and a contact sheet of those charts. Advisory: analysis output from a research and engineering tool, not type-approved navigation equipment and not a safety-of-life service. Every label here is the engine's or the verification matrix's; a kind is MODELLED unless the matrix says otherwise (how a label is earned: the README's Evidence section).

kshana

Kshana · क्षण · the precise instant

Open-source PNT (positioning, navigation and timing) resilience simulator. It drives no radio hardware and transmits nothing: its signal-level output is baseband IQ sample files, and its software GNSS receiver is an analysis tool. Not a flight product.
