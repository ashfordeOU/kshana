Training debrief

What did the crew see, and what was really happening?

Open the instructor log (*.instructor.json) that kshana nmea-scenario writes next to a training stream. It shows the true track against the receiver-reported one, the events that were injected and when, and what the receiver said about its fix, signal, satellites and time. Files you open here are read in this page and never uploaded.

Training and testing only. The streams this log belongs to are synthetic. They must never be fed to a vessel's live navigation systems. Show this log only at the debrief: it tells the crew what was injected. It is not type-approved equipment and says nothing about how any receiver or system would perform against real interference.

        What this page does and does not show

        • The library scenarios are synthetic text with invented positions, dates and tracks. No real vessel, operator or recording is behind them.
        • Heading and log speed (HDT and VBW) stand in for a gyro compass and a Doppler log. No event touches them, so a gap between what GNSS reports and what they say is a debrief cue.
        • The satellite sky is a nominal constellation, not an ephemeris for the stream's date.
        • Nothing here is a measure of how any receiver or system performs against interference.
