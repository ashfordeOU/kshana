Where does a mission's navigation and timing error budget run out?

A mission is a chain of phases: launch, rendezvous, transfer to the Moon, landing, return. Each phase has its own sensors and its own error budget, and each hands its uncertainty to the next. This page shows the budget of the whole chain, phase by phase, and names the weakest link.
