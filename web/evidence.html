Independent oraclepublished reference
Validated
Only with an independent external oracle: published reference vectors, a separate implementation, or measured data held out from fitting. The ledger names the oracle and the test, and the matrix invariant tests enforce the rule.
Modelled
A published model or a documented modelling choice, internally tested, with no independent external oracle yet. Said out loud, not rounded up.
Partner-owned
Spacecraft bus engineering, navigation payload and antenna hardware, quantum payload hardware, and flight-hardware product assurance. Kshana claims none of them; a partner owns them.
