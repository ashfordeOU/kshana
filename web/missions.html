LEO-PNT

A low-orbit navigation layer, scored before it flies.

LEO-PNT (low Earth orbit positioning, navigation and timing): thousands of satellites a few hundred kilometres up, close enough to be heard indoors and moving fast enough to fix a position from Doppler. The engine designs and scores the constellation (the leo-pnt-mega-shell run, in the orbit console below) and follows the signal from its design to the fix, for any system: the cards around this one are recorded runs of those kinds.
